ShopSmart Retail Data Analysis — Week 1



Project Overview

This project analyses ShopSmart Retail data to understand sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, returns, and inventory levels.



Datasets

The analysis used the available Customers, Orders, Order\_Items, Products, Stores, Returns, Suppliers, and Inventory datasets, along with the Employees dataset if included in the original files.



&#x20;Analysis Performed

\- Cleaned and organised the data in Microsoft Excel.

\- Identified primary keys and possible foreign keys.

\- Created PivotTables to analyse revenue by month, store, category, brand, state, region, and customer.

\- Developed KPI summaries and an executive dashboard.

\- Reviewed inventory levels and return patterns.

\- Prepared business findings and recommendations.



Key Findings

\- Beauty was the highest-revenue product category, generating approximately ₦1.76 billion.

\- The South region generated approximately ₦2.42 billion in revenue.

\- December recorded the highest monthly revenue, at approximately ₦660.63 million.



Recommendations

\- Review profitability and consider strategic investment in the Beauty category.

\- Improve inventory monitoring and replenish low-stock items.

\- Use historical sales data to prepare for seasonal sales peaks.



&#x20;Tools Used

Microsoft Excel, including formulas, XLOOKUP, PivotTables, charts, and slicers.
